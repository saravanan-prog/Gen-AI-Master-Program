
## Architecture of GPT, Gemini, and LLaMA

>AI model architecture is the internal design of an AI model. It explains how the model receives information, processes it, and produces an answer.

### How does it work?

1. **User Input:** The user enters a question.

2. **Tokenizer:** The model splits the text into smaller units called tokens.

3. **Embeddings:** The model converts tokens into number representations.

4. **Transformer:** The model processes the tokens and their relationships.

5. **Output Layer:** The model calculates which token is likely to come next.

6. **Final Answer:** The model generates tokens until the answer is complete.
