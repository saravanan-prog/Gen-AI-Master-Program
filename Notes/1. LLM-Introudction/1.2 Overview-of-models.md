## Overview of popular LLM models
## 1. GPT
 - GPT is a family of AI models 
 - that can understand and generate text, help with coding, and solve many language tasks.

## 2. Gemini
- Gemini is a family of AI models designed to work with text and, depending on the model,
  images, audio, video, and other types of input.

## 3. LLaMA (Llama)
- Llama is a family of language models that developers can use to build AI applications.
- Many versions can be downloaded and run locally with suitable hardware and software.

## 4. Mistral
- Mistral is a family of AI models 
- used for tasks such as text generation, question answering, and coding.
-  Some models are available for local use.
