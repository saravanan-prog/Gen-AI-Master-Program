# What is Hallucination?

Hallucination means:
```
 AI gives information that sounds correct but is actually wrong.
```
## Example:

Our document says  : **Library timing: 9 AM to 6 PM**

But the AI answers : **Library timing: 10 AM to 5 PM**

This is a hallucination.
